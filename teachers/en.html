kidvo in one lesson

A 45-minute lesson on using AI wisely and checking what it says.

Ages 7–1345 minutesNo sign-up, no accounts, no cookies

Pick one game

  • Robot Maze
    The AI helper is "100% sure". Some hints are wrong.kidvo.eu/#maze · ages 7–13 · a good first game
  • Secret Keeper
    A friendly AI asks questions. Which ones do you answer?kidvo.eu/#secret-keeper · best at 7–9 · privacy
  • Confident Nonsense
    True and made-up answers, all equally sure.kidvo.eu/#nonsense · best at 10–13 · facts
  • Train the Sorter
    Teach a tiny AI. One-sided examples make it biased.kidvo.eu/#sorter · best at 10–13 · bias

How to run it

One projector, the whole classYou click, the class decides. Before each click, hands up: trust the AI, or check first? Ask a child why.
In pairs, on devicesAny device with a browser. One child plays, the other checks and must agree to each move. Swap every round.

The 45 minutes

  1. 0–5Start. Ask: "Who has used an AI? Was it ever wrong?" Say that the game's AI is pretend: its answers are written in advance, some wrong on purpose.
  2. 5–30Play. One game, 25 minutes. After each level or round, stop briefly: "Was the AI right? How do you know?"
  3. 30–45Talk. Pick three or four questions below. End with one class rule on the board: "Before I trust an AI, I check."

Prepare

  • Try the game on the school network before the lesson. Some school filters block new sites.
  • You need internet and a browser. Nothing to install, no sign-up.
  • English, Hungarian or Romanian: switch at the top of the page.
  • Every game has a one-page parents' guide ("For grown-ups" on its card) to print and send home.
Two modesMost games ask "Who is playing?". Pick Explorer for ages 7–9 or Investigator for 10–13, where the AI's mistakes are harder to spot.

Talk about it

  1. Did the AI ever give a wrong answer? How did you notice? Robot Maze
  2. The AI was always "100% sure". Can someone sound sure and still be wrong? Robot Maze
  3. Which made-up answer almost fooled you? How did you decide what to check? Confident Nonsense
  4. Sunny was very friendly. Does friendly mean safe? What would you never tell an app? Secret Keeper
  5. Why did SORT-1 say a green apple was not an apple? What if an AI only saw one kind of person? Train the Sorter
  6. When is asking an AI worth it, and when can you think it through yourself? any game

After the lesson

Next time you use an AI tool with the class, ask it something you already know and check the answer together. Notice how sure it sounds.

Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no ads. The AI is pretend and connects to nothing. Progress stays in that browser. kidvo counts game plays without names or IDs: which game, how long, how many rounds.
